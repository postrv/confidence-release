## What differentiates Confidence

**One connected explanation from parallel work to a verifiable release decision.** The platform retains immutable contributions, a concrete behavioral witness, repair context from exact failed source, preserved useful goals, exact-source human review and a portable record of what was authorized and observed. Ordinary CI with equally effective checks can find the same bug; the demonstrated distinction is the connected context and decision, not an inability of Git or CI to detect it.
